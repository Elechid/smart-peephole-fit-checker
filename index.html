Smart peephole fit checker

Compare door thickness, existing hole diameter and indoor space for EZVIZ DP2C, DP2 2K and EP4. Leave unmeasured fields blank.

All three accept doors 35–105 mm thick. A measured 15 mm hole passes the diameter check for DP2 2K and EP4. DP2C needs at least 16.5 mm.

EZVIZ lists the screen and base dimensions. Use the actual unit or mounting diagram to locate it, measure the nearest obstruction through the full swing, and check room to fit, remove and charge the screen. Clearance equal to the unit depth still requires confirmation.
